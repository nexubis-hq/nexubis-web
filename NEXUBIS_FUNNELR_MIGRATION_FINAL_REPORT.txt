Final Scorecard website/server behavior:

- Normalises email.
- Finds an existing Funnelr contact by email.
- Creates the contact when missing.
- Updates the existing contact when found.
- Saves Nexubis | Scorecard Report URL.
- Applies Brand: Nexubis.
- Applies Source: Nexubis | Scorecard.
- Applies Trigger: Nexubis | Start Scorecard Sales.
- Does not directly add the contact to a campaign list.
- Does not directly add the contact to a sequence.
- Does not apply History tags.

Repeated submission behavior:

- Updates the same contact.
- Updates the report URL.
- Does not create a duplicate.
- Does not directly restart the sales sequence.

-------------------------------
