Homepage Oxipack Video

Root Cause: Play-Button Positioning
- The actual rendered overlay was not reliably receiving full-frame positioning.
- The overlay collapsed to the 64px play button instead of covering the whole media frame.
- Because the collapsed overlay participated in the media wrapper layout, the button and pulse appeared at the bottom edge of the media frame.

Root Cause: Blank Poster / Media Frame
- The approved local poster at /assets/work/oxipack/hero-poster.webp is effectively white/blank.
- Relying only on the native video poster therefore produced a white-looking media frame.

Component and CSS Correction
- components/OxipackProofVideo.tsx now owns the critical frame, media, overlay, and button geometry directly.
- The media frame remains position: relative, 16 / 9, rounded, and overflow hidden.
- The poster and video layers fill the media frame with object-fit: contain.
- The control overlay fills the media frame and uses grid centering.
- The play button and pulse share the same centered button element.
- The approved poster remains as fallback.
- The paused approved R2 video seeks to 1.5s after metadata loads so the initial state shows a visible Oxipack frame before click-to-play.
- Playback remains click-to-play, muted, playsInline, preload="metadata", and without native controls.
